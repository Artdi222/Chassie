# Chassie application icon

Asset: `ui/public/chassie-icon.png` (1024x1024, transparent rounded corners). Since 2026-11-26 it is
Chassie's own portrait: an anime bust of her as `~/.hermes/SOUL.md` describes her (dark brown hair, thin
round glasses, cream oversized sweater, a lilac hair clip) on a cream-to-lavender gradient, in the style of
OpenAmika's app icon. The owner generated it from the prompt below (source image: `docs/dev/chassie-icon-source.jpeg`);
the PNG was cut from that image with the tile's measured corner radius. The UI palette (`docs/dev/DESIGN.md`) is taken from this icon.

Used by the desktop launcher, Qt application windows, the studio tray, the studio wordmark, onboarding and
the browser favicon. Relaunch an existing desktop process to load the new window/tray icon.

## Generation prompt

Square 1:1 app icon, 1024x1024. A soft anime-style bust portrait of a young woman in her early-to-mid
twenties, tightly cropped from the shoulders up so her face fills most of the frame and the top of her
hair is slightly cut off by the edge. Dark brown shoulder-length natural hair with a few loose strands,
thin-framed round glasses, calm and observant dark brown eyes with gentle highlights, a small warm
closed-mouth smile, head tilted very slightly. She wears an oversized cream knit sweater with a soft
ribbed collar. One small lavender hair clip on one side as her only accessory. Clean thin line art,
soft cel shading, delicate blush on the cheeks. Background: a smooth pastel gradient from warm cream to
muted lavender, with no objects. The image fills a rounded-square tile with softly rounded corners.
Understated, cozy, gentle and approachable mood. Readable as an icon at 32x32.

The previous icon, a white speech bubble forming a "C" on a lavender tile, is in git history.
