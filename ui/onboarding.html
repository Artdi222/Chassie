Welcome

A companion that lives on your desktop.

Chassie is an animated character you can talk to. She answers with a voice, remembers what matters to you, and keeps you company while you work.

  • Talk or typeUse the microphone or the chat box, in English or Indonesian.
  • She remembers youClear facts and preferences are kept as notes you can read and edit.
  • Stays on this computerHer memory, voice, and camera never leave your machine.

Setup takes about a minute. First, she needs a body.

Step 2 of 4

Choose her look.

Chassie uses Live2D models — the same kind VTubers use. Add the model you downloaded; no need to unzip it.

Where do I get a Live2D model?
  1. Find a free or paid model on BOOTH or nizima. VTube Studio models work too.
  2. Download it. It usually comes as a .zip file.
  3. Drop the .zip above. Chassie finds the model inside and skips extras like PSD artwork.

Chassie needs a Cubism 3 or 4 model: one with a .model3.json file. Please respect each model's licence.
